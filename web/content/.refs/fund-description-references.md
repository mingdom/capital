## **Pershing Square Capital Management / Pershing Square Holdings — Bill Ackman**

**Official Fund Objective (Pershing Square Holdings, Ltd.)**:

> *“Pershing Square Holdings, Ltd. … is an investment holding company structured as a closed-ended fund principally engaged in the business of acquiring and holding significant (but generally not controlling) positions in a concentrated number of large capitalization companies. PSH’s objective is to **maximize its long-term compound annual rate of growth in intrinsic value per share**.”* ([Pershing Square Holdings, Ltd.][1])

**Investment Strategy (SEC Filing Summary)**:

> *“The Fund will seek to achieve its investment objective by investing principally in common stocks … believed to exhibit **significant valuation discrepancies between current trading prices and intrinsic business value, often with a catalyst for value recognition**.”* ([SEC][2])

**Public Positioning of the Firm (Wikipedia Overview)**:

> *“Pershing Square Capital Management… is an American hedge fund based in New York City. It specializes in **activist investments**, using influence over company management through the purchase of shares and by exerting pressure on management.”* ([Wikipedia][3])

**Activity-Focused Statement (Industry Description)**:

> *“Pershing Square… is a **concentrated, research-intensive fundamental value investor** in long and occasionally short investments in the public markets.”* ([hcsandiego.clubs.harvard.edu][4])

**Characteristic Investor Language (Common Industry Source)**:

> *“Bill Ackman’s Pershing Square… takes **substantial positions in high-quality businesses that he believes are undervalued or underperforming**.”* ([Gotrade][5])

# **Key Language Patterns (From Real Funds)**

Share these common structures with your copywriter so they can see how institutions *frame their mission and posture*:

### **1. Objective-First Statements**

* *“…maximize its long-term compound annual rate of growth in intrinsic value per share.”* (Pershing Square) ([Pershing Square Holdings, Ltd.][1])
* *“…employs mathematical and statistical methods…”* (Renaissance) ([Pershing Square Holdings, Ltd.][1])

### **2. Approach/Methodology**

* *“…investing in companies with valuation discrepancies… with a catalyst for value recognition.”* (Pershing Square) ([SEC][2])
* *“…structure portfolios with uncorrelated investment returns…”* (Bridgewater) ([Wikipedia][3])

### **3. Philosophy + Role**

* *“…specializes in activist investments, using influence over company management…”* (Pershing Square) ([Wikipedia][3])
* *“…intellectually vibrant culture… data-driven approach…”* (Renaissance) ([Pershing Square Holdings, Ltd.][1])
* *“…value investing manager… risk management…”* (Baupost) ([Wikipedia][3])

### **4. Tone & Cadence**

* Objective, not boastful
* Avoids performance promises
* Focuses on **how** decisions are made and **why** they pursue the approach
* Uses durable concepts (value, intrinsic growth, risk allocation, concentrated positions)

# **What Your Copywriter Should Extract From These Excerpts**

Your copywriter should **not**:

* Describe internal risk rules (e.g., position limits, betas, convexity budgets) in the public narrative
* Discuss instruments (options, leverage) or tactical constructs
* Include numerical thresholds

Your copywriter **should** use language that:

* Frames the fund’s *mission* and *approach* in terms similar to the excerpts above
* Emphasizes **long-term value creation and disciplined methodology**
* Uses durable verbs and nouns (compound growth, intrinsic value, research-intensive, concentrated, risk-aware)
