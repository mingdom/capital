# About Mingdom Capital

## Who I Am

I'm a software engineer who achieved financial independence in my 30s and now manage my family fund. I've been active in the stock market since 2009, navigating multiple market cycles from the Great Financial Crisis recovery through today's AI-driven markets.

After delivering **72% returns in 2023**, I made a decision: go public. In February 2024, I started sharing my portfolio transparently—not for marketing, but for accountability. Public scrutiny keeps me sharp. Every position, every trade, every decision is on the record.

This isn't a side project. It's my capital, my family's future, and my reputation on the line.

## Why This Matters

**Skin in the game.** I'm not managing other people's money with a 2-and-20 fee structure. This is my wealth, invested alongside anyone who follows my strategy. When I win, I win. When I'm wrong, I feel it.

**Transparency over opacity.** Most funds hide behind 30-day disclosure lags and vague commentary. I publish holdings, performance, and reasoning in real-time. You see what I see.

**Long-term compounding over short-term noise.** I'm not chasing quarterly performance bonuses. I'm building generational wealth through concentrated positions in exceptional businesses.
